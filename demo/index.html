The coaching problem

Players need feedback they can use in the next session

Movement

A practice video captures reach, posture and joint positions. Players need help turning those observations into a training focus.

Physical context

A timestamped heart-rate reading adds context to that clip. It can inform a review without proving why a technique changed.

The experience

One practice clip becomes measured feedback

Use a drill card to give the next session a clear focus.

Trust in the result

Every reading and session has an owner

Player access

Expiring credentials bind access to a player. Session results, Watch data and live events enforce that ownership.

Measurement provenance

Actual Health sample time stays separate from receipt time. Stale samples fail validation. Missing results and model fallback remain visible.

External booking or invite actions require explicit approval. Professional reference profiles remain labelled illustrative and unvalidated.
