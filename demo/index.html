The problem
Playing is easy. Getting better isn’t.
No one watching
You can’t see your own form, and regular lessons are expensive.
Tips, but no plan
A tutorial shows a drill once. Nothing turns it into what you practise this week.
Admin in the way
Courts, kit and scheduling eat the time you’d spend practising.
The idea
Saint runs your improvement plan.
Tell it what you want to fix, like “sort out my backhand”, and Saint gets your form reviewed, plans the drills and clears the admin out of the way.
What Tempo does
Built around getting better.
1

2
Plans your drills
Builds a week of drills around those fixes and puts the sessions in your calendar.
3
Clears the admin
Books the court and restocks your kit, so nothing gets between you and practice.
